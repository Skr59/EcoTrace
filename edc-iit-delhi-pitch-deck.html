How it works · MRV

Measurement you can actually trust

STEP 01

Map the land

Farmer draws the field boundary on a live satellite map. Area & perimeter are computed instantly in hectares.

STEP 02

Survey from the air

A survey drone flies an auto-generated grid over the plot, capturing canopy & coverage with a live FPV feed.

STEP 03

Convert to credits

Emission factors + field data + live prices → verified credits, listed on the marketplace automatically.
